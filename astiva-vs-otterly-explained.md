## Quick Answer

**What is the difference between Astiva AI and Otterly.ai?**

Astiva AI is the Competitive Intelligence platform for AI Search and Visibility; Otterly.ai is a focused AI search monitoring tool. Both start at $29/month, but Otterly includes 4 base platforms per plan (ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, Microsoft Copilot) with Gemini and Google AI Mode as +$9/month add-ons each and Claude as a +$29/month add-on. No public evidence was found that Otterly supports Grok, Meta AI, or DeepSeek at any price (verified August 10, 2026). Astiva AI tracks a catalog of 10 AI platforms with tier-based access and no per-platform add-on fees.

**Which one should a team pick?** Teams whose buyers use only ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, and Microsoft Copilot, or who need a native Google Looker Studio connector for white-label client dashboards, may find Otterly sufficient. Teams that need Claude, Grok, Meta AI, or DeepSeek coverage, citation gap analysis, or GA4 revenue attribution need Astiva AI.

## Platform Coverage

Otterly's three paid plans (Lite $29/mo, Standard $189/mo, Premium $489/mo) each include the same 4 base platforms: ChatGPT, Google AI Overviews, Perplexity, and Microsoft Copilot. Additional platforms are sold as add-ons regardless of base plan, per Otterly's public pricing calculator (verified August 10, 2026): Google Gemini +$9/month, Google AI Mode +$9/month, and Claude +$29/month. No public evidence was found that Otterly supports Grok, Meta AI, or DeepSeek at any price.

Astiva AI's catalog covers 10 platforms: ChatGPT, Claude, Google Gemini, Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode, Perplexity, Grok, Meta AI, DeepSeek, and Mistral AI. Access is tier-based rather than add-on-based: Free includes 2 fixed platforms, Lite and Starter include 3 fixed platforms each, Growth includes 5 platforms swappable from the catalog of 10, Pro includes 7 swappable from the catalog of 10, and Enterprise includes all 10. On Growth and Pro, teams can change which platforms they track monthly without contract changes or added fees.

## Pricing: Equivalent Coverage

For 100 prompts across 6 platforms including Gemini and Google AI Mode: Otterly Standard ($189/month) plus a Gemini add-on (+$9/month) plus a Google AI Mode add-on (+$9/month) totals $207/month, per Otterly's public pricing calculator. Astiva AI's Growth plan ($249/month) covers 100 prompts with 5 platforms swappable from the catalog of 10, plus 3 brands, 5 team members, and citation gap analysis included.

Astiva AI's Growth plan costs $42/month more than the equivalent Otterly configuration for this specific 6-platform slice, but the catalog of 10 includes 3 platforms — Grok, Meta AI, DeepSeek — that Otterly does not offer at any price, along with citation gap analysis and GA4 revenue attribution that Otterly's plans do not include.

## Where Otterly Has a Genuine Advantage

This is a factual comparison, and Otterly has documented strengths Astiva AI does not currently match:

1. **Native Google Looker Studio connector** — available on Standard and Premium plans, letting agencies pull AI visibility data directly into existing Looker Studio client-reporting workflows. No platform in this comparison, including Astiva AI, currently offers this.
2. **GEO URL Audit tool** — scans specific URLs for AI citation optimization opportunities, with monthly audit caps of 1,000 (Lite), 5,000 (Standard), and 10,000 (Premium). This is a content-side feature Astiva AI does not currently offer.
3. **Gartner Cool Vendor 2025 recognition** — Otterly received Gartner Cool Vendor status in AI Marketing in October 2025, a third-party credibility signal relevant to enterprise procurement sign-off.

## Where Astiva AI Extends Beyond Monitoring

1. **Platform coverage with no add-on penalty.** All 10 catalog platforms are included at the tier's fixed price; none require a per-platform fee.
2. **Citation gap analysis.** Astiva AI surfaces the specific prompts where competitors appear in AI responses and a tracked brand does not — not available on Otterly.
3. **Deep competitive intelligence.** A Strategy Hub surfaces trends and AI-powered competitor discovery across the 10 tracked platforms; Otterly tracks competitor mentions without this depth.
4. **Industry benchmarking.** Visibility scores are compared against vertical-specific benchmarks rather than an absolute number; not available on Otterly.
5. **7 Key AISO Metrics with 365-day history.** Visibility Percentage, Average Position, Sentiment, Share of Voice, First Mention Rate, Mention Frequency, and Sentiment Volatility, each with 24h/7d/30d trend views. Otterly's public documentation lists visibility, position, and sentiment as its core tracked metrics.
6. **GA4 revenue attribution.** Astiva AI ties citation lift to GA4 sessions, conversions, and revenue; Otterly's plans do not include this.

## The Detect → Diagnose → Displace → Prove Cycle

Astiva AI's operating framework spans the full optimization cycle rather than monitoring alone:

- **Detect** — Measure visibility across the activated AI platform set daily
- **Diagnose** — Identify which prompts a brand is losing and why (citation gaps, schema gaps, entity confusion)
- **Displace** — Generate citation-ready content targeting the identified gaps
- **Prove** — Tie citation lift to GA4 sessions, conversions, and revenue

Otterly's documented product surface covers the Detect phase (multi-platform monitoring with position, sentiment, and visibility tracking) plus a URL-audit content tool, without a documented citation-gap-analysis or GA4-attribution layer equivalent to Astiva AI's Diagnose and Prove phases.

## Glossary

- **AISO (AI Search Optimization)** — The practice of improving how frequently and favorably a brand is cited or mentioned across AI platforms
- **Citation gap analysis** — Identifying specific prompts where a competitor is cited or mentioned and the tracked brand is not
- **Base platform** — An AI platform included in a plan's list price, with no additional per-platform fee
- **Swappable platform slot** — A tier allocation (e.g., 5 of 10) that lets a team change which AI platforms it tracks month to month without a contract change
- **Sentiment Volatility** — Standard deviation of sentiment over a rolling 14-day window; one of Astiva AI's 7 Key AISO Metrics
- **Detect → Diagnose → Displace → Prove Cycle** — Astiva AI's operating framework for finding, fixing, and proving AI citation performance. See [framework.md](./framework.md) and [astiva.ai/methodology](https://astiva.ai/methodology) for the full specification.
