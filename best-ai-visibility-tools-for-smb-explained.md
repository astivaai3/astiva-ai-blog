## The Four Decision Questions

The SMB tool-selection decision tree reduces to four questions, ordered by elimination strength:

1. **What is your monthly budget band?** Under $50/mo eliminates Profound, AthenaHQ, Otterly Standard, and Peec AI Pro.
2. **Do you need multi-engine coverage on day one?** If yes, Profound Starter (ChatGPT only), SE Visible (two engines live), and Otterly Lite at $29 with paid add-ons are weakened candidates.
3. **Do you need content generation included?** If yes, the list narrows to Astiva AI (from Starter $99) and GrackerAI (from Starter $39).
4. **Do you need GA4 revenue attribution?** If yes, the answer is Astiva AI Growth ($249) or AthenaHQ Self-Serve ($295).

## What SMB Teams Get Wrong

Three patterns separate SMBs that get value from these tools from SMBs that buy a dashboard and watch it gather dust:

1. **Single-engine validation creates a false-positive read.** ChatGPT is the most-used engine, but Perplexity, Gemini, Claude, and Google AI Mode each attract distinct buyer segments. Three months of ChatGPT-only data produces a confident-looking dataset that is also incomplete.
2. **Per-engine add-on math destroys the headline price.** A team tracking 50 prompts across 5 engines, with Gemini and AI Mode as add-ons, can be at $80+/mo before realizing it.
3. **Measurement without action is theatre.** The Detect phase is the cheapest to deliver. The tools with a closed loop (Astiva AI Detect → Diagnose → Displace → Prove Cycle, GrackerAI content generation, AthenaHQ Action Center) produce outcomes; the pure-measurement tools produce reports.

## Glossary

- **AI Visibility** — Frequency and quality of brand citation across AI search platforms
- **AISO (AI Search Optimization)** — The discipline of measuring and improving AI-driven brand visibility
- **Multi-engine coverage** — Tracking brand citation across more than one AI platform within a single tool
- **Per-engine add-on** — Pricing model where each additional AI platform costs extra (Otterly, Peec AI); contrasts with catalogue-access models (Astiva AI, LLM Pulse) where the entry price covers a defined set
- **Citation gap analysis** — Identification of prompts where competitors are cited and the brand is not
- **Closed-loop tool** — A tool that combines measurement, diagnosis, content generation, and attribution; contrasts with pure-measurement tools
- **GA4 revenue attribution** — Native integration that ties AI citation lift to sessions, conversions, and revenue inside Google Analytics 4
- **Validation buyer / Production SMB / Depth buyer** — The three SMB budget bands defining the 2026 category
