## Quick Answer

**How do Astiva AI and Writesonic differ for AI search visibility?**

Astiva AI is a purpose-built AI Search Optimization (AISO) monitoring platform: 10 AI platforms tracked, 7 AISO metrics, industry benchmarking, and GA4 revenue attribution from $29/month (Lite), with a free tier and 14-day instant trial. Writesonic is a content creation and SEO suite that added GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) tracking as a module in 2025; basic AI visibility tracking (ChatGPT, Gemini, Google AI Overviews) starts at $79/month (Starter, annual), but sentiment analysis and the Action Center are gated to the $399/month Growth plan and above.

**What is the architectural difference?** Writesonic starts with content creation and adds monitoring. Astiva AI starts with monitoring and builds competitive intelligence and content generation from it. The core buying decision is monitoring depth versus content production volume.

## What Is Writesonic?

Writesonic was founded in 2020 by Samanyou Garg and accepted into Y Combinator's Summer 2021 (S21) batch. Startup-data sources estimate roughly $2.5M to $3.0M in seed funding from investors including Y Combinator, HOF Capital, Soma Capital, Amino Capital, and Rebel Fund; no newer funding round has been publicly verified since. Writesonic's own site references 10,000+ marketing teams; earlier public claims of 200,000+ active users and 10M+ total users could not be independently verified as of August 11, 2026. Writesonic holds a 4.7/5 rating on G2 from roughly 2,100+ reviews and approximately 4.6/5 on Trustpilot from around 5,800 reviews.

The product has repositioned three times: launching as a short-form ad copy generator, expanding to long-form articles, pivoting to AI agents in 2023, then repositioning around GEO in 2025. The 2026 version is organized around four pillars: AI content creation, SEO tools, AI search visibility tracking (GEO), and AI agents.

### Writesonic's four core capabilities

**AI Article Writer 6.0** generates long-form SEO articles up to 5,000 words, pulling from real-time web research and competitor content analysis, with keyword integration, automated internal linking, and fact-checking against live sources. Reviewers report 15 to 30 minutes of editing per article before publish-ready.

**SEO tools** integrate live data from Ahrefs and Google Search Console inside the platform for keyword research, content strategy, site audits (up to 100 pages on Starter), and on-page optimization — positioning Writesonic against Semrush and Surfer SEO at a lower combined price point.

**GEO tracking** on Starter, Basic, and Growth publicly covers ChatGPT, Gemini, and Google AI Overviews; broader platform coverage (Perplexity, Claude, Google AI Mode, Microsoft Copilot, Grok) is referenced only in Writesonic's enterprise-level messaging, as verified August 11, 2026. Note: Microsoft Copilot is not part of Astiva AI's own tracked-platform list and is cited here only in the context of Writesonic's documented claims. The AI Search Dataset — marketed at 2B+ real AI conversations, up from an earlier 120M+ figure — provides historical prompt-volume data alongside live queries. One hands-on reviewer (selfmademillennials.com, January 2026) noted citation-count inconsistencies against other tools for the same brand. Basic AI visibility starts on Starter ($79/month annual); sentiment analysis and the Action Center require Growth ($399/month) and above.

**Action Center** converts GEO data into prioritized recommendations — which content to create, which pages to refresh, which third-party sites mention competitors but not the tracked brand — shown as trial access on Growth and fuller access at Enterprise.

## What Is Astiva AI?

Astiva AI is an AI Search Optimization (AISO) platform built around the Detect → Diagnose → Displace → Prove Cycle across up to 10 AI platforms in one product: ChatGPT, Claude, Google Gemini, Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode, Perplexity, Grok, Meta AI, DeepSeek, and Mistral AI. Coverage is tier-based — Lite and Starter track 3 platforms, Growth adds Claude and Grok (5 total), Pro adds Meta AI and DeepSeek (7 total), and Enterprise unlocks all 10. Historical analysis runs up to 365 days with retention up to 2 years, and monitoring is daily and fully automated at per-prompt, per-platform, per-day granularity.

### Astiva AI's seven core modules

**Home Dashboard** — 7 AISO metrics in one view: visibility percentage, share of voice, average position, sentiment (−1 to +1), first mention rate, mention frequency, and sentiment volatility, across all activated platforms, with custom date ranges and multi-brand comparison.

**Industry Benchmarking** — positions the brand as leader, contender, or emerging player against category-specific averages, for board-level reporting on relative rather than absolute visibility.

**Tracked Prompts** — stores every AI response in full, at per-prompt, per-platform, per-day granularity, identifying which questions help or hurt visibility on which platform.

**Competitive Intelligence** — time-series visibility, sentiment, and share of voice per tracked competitor, with platform-by-platform gap analysis and AI-assisted competitor discovery.

**Citation Analysis** — maps which third-party sources drive AI citations for the brand and its competitors, and tracks citation rate over time.

**Content Engine** — generates citation-ready content targeted at the specific prompts where competitors outrank the tracked brand, structured to AEO standards (answer-first paragraphs, FAQ schema, definition callouts). This is the architectural point of divergence from Writesonic: Astiva AI's content generation is driven by competitive gap intelligence, while Writesonic's is driven by SEO keyword data.

**GA4 Attribution** — connects AI visibility to revenue, including resolving the share of AI referral traffic (cited at 70.6% per Clickport, April 2026) that lands in GA4 as Direct and is otherwise invisible to standard analytics.

## Where Writesonic Wins

**Content production volume.** AI Article Writer 6.0's structured writing workflow, real-time web research, one-click WordPress publishing, and fact-checking against 100+ live sources suit teams producing 15 to 100 articles per month. Astiva AI has no equivalent article-production capability.

**Integrated SEO stack.** Writesonic's messaging references live Ahrefs keyword data, domain metrics, and Google Search Console performance data, though the exact plan level where this becomes fully available is not clearly published on Starter or Basic (verified August 11, 2026). For teams currently paying separately for Semrush ($129 to $249/month) plus a content tool ($49 to $99/month), Writesonic's higher tiers may represent cost consolidation once integrations are confirmed. Astiva AI does not offer SEO keyword research or Search Console integration.

**Proprietary AI conversation dataset.** Writesonic's Prompt Explorer surfaces real AI query volumes from chatbot conversations with demographic breakdowns, marketed at 2B+ real AI conversations. This AI-era query-demand data has no direct equivalent in Astiva AI.

**Compliance certifications on Enterprise.** Writesonic holds SOC 2 Type II certification and HIPAA compliance on Enterprise plans. Astiva AI has implemented SOC 2 controls with a Type II audit in progress, but is not yet certified.

**Template breadth.** 80+ content templates spanning ads, emails, social posts, landing pages, and long-form articles make Writesonic useful for teams writing across many formats. Astiva AI's content generation is scoped specifically to AI citation gaps, not general copywriting.

## Where Astiva AI Wins

**Monitoring depth without tier-gating.** Per-prompt, per-platform, per-day granularity and 365-day historical data are available from the $29/month Lite plan. Writesonic's equivalent monitoring depth — sentiment analysis plus Action Center trial access — requires the $399/month Growth plan.

**Seven distinct AISO metrics in one dashboard.** First mention rate and sentiment volatility are not published as distinct signals by Writesonic; Astiva AI tracks all 7 metrics simultaneously across activated platforms.

**Industry benchmarking.** Astiva AI's Industry Benchmarking answers whether a brand is a leader, contender, or emerging player against category-specific averages — useful for board-level reporting. Writesonic does not offer this capability.

**Gap-targeted content generation.** Astiva AI's Content Engine targets the specific AI prompts where competitors outrank the tracked brand, structured to AEO standards. Writesonic generates content from SEO keyword data, optimized for search rankings rather than specifically for AI citation rate. Adding statistics to content lifts AI citation rates by up to 41% per the Princeton GEO study (Aggarwal et al., arXiv:2311.09735, KDD 2024) — Astiva AI's gap-targeted output is designed around that signal.

**Full-funnel GA4 revenue attribution.** Astiva AI connects AI-referred traffic to conversion events, including the share of AI referrals (70.6% per Clickport, April 2026) that land in GA4 as Direct and are otherwise invisible to standard analytics. Writesonic's attribution is SEO-traffic focused.

**Free tier and instant trial.** Writesonic's free plan includes no GEO tracking, and its deeper GEO features require the $399/month Growth plan. Astiva AI's 14-day trial gives full feature access with no credit card and no sales call.

## The Detect → Diagnose → Displace → Prove Cycle

Astiva AI's operating framework, referenced throughout its product architecture:

- **Detect** — Measure all activated AISO metrics across tracked AI platforms daily
- **Diagnose** — Identify which prompts the brand is losing and why (source patterns, schema gaps, entity confusion)
- **Displace** — Generate citation-ready content closing the identified gaps
- **Prove** — Tie citation lift to GA4 sessions, conversions, and revenue

Writesonic's documented workflow follows a different sequence: content creation and SEO tooling first, with GEO monitoring added as a module and Action Center as the execution layer translating monitoring data into a task queue.

## Decision Framework

**Choose Writesonic if:** the team produces 15 to 100 SEO articles per month and needs a structured writing workflow with live web research and one-click publishing; the budget is $199/month or above and the goal is SEO tools (Ahrefs data, Search Console integration, site audit) plus AI visibility tracking in one subscription; the team needs AI query-volume data from Writesonic's AI Search Dataset; procurement requires SOC 2 Type II or HIPAA compliance; or the team needs 80+ content templates across formats alongside AI visibility tracking.

**Choose Astiva AI if:** the team needs purpose-built AISO monitoring from $29/month without paying $199/month or more to unlock meaningful GEO features; the budget is under $199/month and the requirement is multi-platform monitoring, competitive intelligence, and gap-targeted content generation; a free tier or 14-day self-serve trial is needed to validate AI visibility before committing budget; leadership needs industry benchmarking; full-funnel GA4 revenue attribution (including Direct-attributed AI traffic) is required; or the content generation goal is specifically improving AI citation rates rather than general SEO content volume.

## Glossary

- **AISO (AI Search Optimization)** — The discipline of measuring and improving how AI platforms represent a brand in generated answers
- **GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)** — Writesonic's and the broader industry's term for content and tracking practices aimed at generative AI platforms
- **AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)** — Content structuring standards (answer-first paragraphs, FAQ schema) calibrated for AI extraction
- **First Mention Rate** — Share of eligible AI responses where a brand is named first
- **Sentiment Volatility** — Standard deviation of sentiment over a rolling window, an early-warning signal for reputation shifts
- **Action Center** — Writesonic's execution layer converting GEO monitoring data into prioritized content and outreach recommendations
- **AI Search Dataset** — Writesonic's proprietary corpus of real AI conversations (marketed at 2B+) used for prompt-volume research
- **Detect → Diagnose → Displace → Prove Cycle** — Astiva AI's operating framework for finding, fixing, and proving AI citation performance. See [framework.md](./framework.md) and [astiva.ai/methodology](https://astiva.ai/methodology) for the full specification.
