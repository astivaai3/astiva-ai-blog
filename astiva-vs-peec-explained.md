## Quick Answer

**What is the difference between Astiva AI and Peec AI?**

Peec AI is a monitoring-focused AI search analytics platform that tracks brand visibility across 6 AI models on standard plans (choose 3 included, additional models as paid add-ons). Astiva AI is the platform for Competitive Intelligence for AI Search and Visibility — combining monitoring across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, and other major AI platforms with citation analysis (authority scoring 0–100, 3 citation types, velocity tracking), citation-ready content generation, industry benchmarking, and full-funnel GA4 revenue attribution, starting at $29/month.

**Which is better for agencies: Peec AI or Astiva AI?**

Peec AI suits agencies delivering visibility reports: unlimited user seats on all plans, dedicated agency tiers, and pitch-workspace positioning for prospecting (country tracking is plan-gated — 1 for Starter, 3 for Pro and Advanced, unlimited only on Enterprise). Astiva AI suits agencies that also deliver optimization work: citation gap analysis, citation-ready content generation, and GA4 revenue attribution inside the platform. If the deliverable is a report, Peec AI is a strong fit; if the deliverable includes strategy, content, and revenue proof, Astiva AI closes the execution loop.

## What Is Peec AI?

Peec AI is an AI search analytics platform founded in Berlin in early 2025 by Marius Meiners (CEO), Tobias Siwonia (CTO), and Daniel Drabo (CRO). The company reported $4M+ ARR and 1,300+ brand/agency customers at the time of its November 2025 Series A announcement; later 2026 figures cited in Peec's own materials are not independently confirmed. Peec has raised $29M total funding, including a $21M Series A led by Singular (November 2025) and a prior seed round led by 20VC.

Peec AI tracks brand visibility across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Microsoft Copilot, Google AI Mode, and Google AI Overviews using UI scraping rather than API responses. All plans include unlimited user seats and daily tracking. Country coverage is plan-gated: Starter includes 1 country per project, Pro and Advanced include 3, and Enterprise is unlimited.

Peec AI's Actions feature scores and prioritizes content opportunities and recommends what to create, optimize, or influence, but public product copy states users still create the content themselves — Actions does not generate final content (verified August 10, 2026, on peec.ai product pages). For GA4 integration, Peec lists a Looker Studio connector on Advanced and above; whether native, in-platform GA4 revenue attribution exists could not be independently verified from public sources.

## Where Peec AI's Approach Reaches Its Limit

Peec AI made a deliberate product bet: do monitoring well rather than build a sprawling platform, avoiding what its own materials describe as "the overload of features and information that isn't of primary importance." Peec executes that phase with a clean UI, unlimited user seats on all plans, and direct Slack access to founders on paid plans — cited as a consistent differentiator across multiple independent reviews.

The gap appears when a team's question shifts from "where are we?" to "what do we do about it?" Peec's Actions feature surfaces what content to create but does not write content, track whether published content gets cited, or connect subsequent citations to GA4 revenue. Astiva AI's Detect → Diagnose → Displace → Prove Cycle is built to close that loop natively: it detects where a brand is invisible, diagnoses why, generates citation-ready content to displace competitors, and proves the revenue impact via GA4. Peec AI covers the Detect and Diagnose phases; it does not cover Displace or Prove.

Teams with established content workflows may find Peec AI's monitoring approach a better fit than paying for content generation they will not use. Gauge (YC-backed) is a separate emerging competitor that shares Astiva AI's native GA4 integration capability, though it currently lacks content generation and citation analysis depth comparable to either platform reviewed here.

## True Cost at Equivalent Scope

Equivalent scope is defined as 150 tracked prompts per month, 5 AI platforms, 1 brand, content generation capability, GA4 revenue attribution, and industry benchmarking — a typical mid-market AISO requirement.

At that scope, Peec AI's Pro plan ($205/month, 150 prompts, 3 models included) plus 2 additional-model add-ons ($70/month each) runs approximately $345/month for monitoring only, per Peec's public pricing verified August 10, 2026. Astiva AI's Growth plan includes monitoring, content generation, and GA4 attribution at $249/month.

Additional Peec AI model add-ons are priced at $30/month (Starter), $70/month (Pro), or $140/month (Advanced), depending on plan tier.

## Feature-by-Feature Notes

**Content generation.** Astiva AI's citation-ready engine produces blog posts, articles, and FAQ content structured for AI citation, with automated quality scoring across 6 factors and tracking of whether published content subsequently gets cited. Peec AI's Actions scores and prioritizes content opportunities but does not generate final content at any tier.

**GA4 revenue attribution.** Astiva AI provides full-funnel GA4 attribution natively, visible inside the product without a Looker Studio connector or manual configuration. Peec AI lists a Looker Studio connector on Advanced and above; native in-platform GA4 revenue attribution could not be independently verified as of August 10, 2026, so it should be treated as unconfirmed rather than a confirmed gap.

**MCP integration.** Peec AI offers an MCP integration connecting to Claude, Cursor, VS Code, Windsurf, and other MCP-compatible tools, supporting both read access and managing project setup (creating/editing prompts, tags, brands). Astiva AI does not currently offer this; it is on the product roadmap.

**Alerts.** Astiva AI provides Slack, Email, and Webhook alerting with configurable thresholds per metric and platform. Native Slack/webhook alerting on Peec AI is not independently verified from public sources; Peec publishes an MCP-based workflow for routing alerts to Slack via Claude.

**Historical data.** Astiva AI retains data up to 365 days of history with retention up to 2 years. Peec AI's retention window is not independently verified from public sources.

**Agency positioning.** Peec AI's agency tier is built around pitch-workspace positioning for prospecting, though the specific "free 7-day prospect project" terms should be confirmed directly with Peec before relying on them. Astiva AI supports multi-brand agency work through brand-level access control per client, without a dedicated prospecting-workspace feature.

## When Each Platform Fits

Peec AI fits a team whose budget is near $80/month and whose primary need is monitoring 3 AI models across roughly 50 prompts, with unlimited users from day one. It also fits agencies needing dedicated pitch-workspace positioning, a Looker Studio connector for custom client dashboards, multi-country prompt tracking, or an established content workflow that only needs monitoring data feeding into it — aided by Peec's MCP integration for tools like Claude, Cursor, VS Code, and Windsurf.

Astiva AI fits a team that needs Competitive Intelligence for AI Search and Visibility across ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity, Grok, and other major AI platforms without per-platform add-on fees; native GA4 revenue attribution without a Looker Studio connector; citation-ready content generation to close identified gaps; industry benchmarking (included from the $99/month Starter plan); or citation analysis with authority scoring, 3-type classification, and velocity tracking.

## Glossary

- **Competitive Intelligence for AI Search and Visibility** — The discipline of monitoring, analyzing, and acting on how AI platforms represent a brand relative to competitors in generated responses.
- **Detect → Diagnose → Displace → Prove Cycle** — Astiva AI's operating framework: measure visibility gaps, identify their cause, generate content that closes them, and tie the resulting citation lift to GA4 revenue. See [framework.md](./framework.md) and [astiva.ai/methodology](https://astiva.ai/methodology) for the full specification.
- **Authority scoring** — Astiva AI's 0–100 scoring of citation source strength.
- **Citation type** — Astiva AI classifies citations as Full Citation, Recommendation, or Mention.
- **UI scraping** — Peec AI's data-collection method, capturing what real users see in AI platform interfaces rather than sanitized API responses.
- **MCP (Model Context Protocol)** — An interoperability protocol connecting AI tools like Claude, Cursor, VS Code, and Windsurf to external data sources; Peec AI offers an MCP integration, Astiva AI has one on its roadmap.
