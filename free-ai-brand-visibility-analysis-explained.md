The Astiva AI free AI brand visibility analysis runs your brand across ChatGPT and Perplexity on real buyer-intent queries and returns a brand visibility score, citation breakdown, competitor share of voice, and a PDF report in under 5 minutes. The result is a baseline read on whether the brand has any AI visibility footprint, ready for the Diagnose phase of an AISO program.

## What the Free Analysis Tests

### Platforms

- **ChatGPT** (OpenAI)
- **Perplexity**

The two-platform scope is deliberate: ChatGPT is the highest-volume AI assistant; Perplexity has the strongest per-citation weight per the Averi March 2026 study. Together they cover the most-cited 11% intersection plus the largest exclusive citation pools each platform contributes.

The full 10-platform catalogue (adding Claude, Google Gemini, Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode, Grok, Meta AI, DeepSeek, Mistral AI) activates on paid tiers.

### Prompt set

10 lifetime prompts in the Free tier. The prompts are buyer-intent queries the user can configure or import. Typical patterns:

- "Best [category] for [use case]" — comparison queries
- "[Competitor] alternatives" — alternative-search queries
- "How to do [task] with [category]" — procedural queries
- "[Category] pricing comparison" — commercial queries

The Free tier's lifetime cap exists to keep the surface honest as a category-validation tool rather than an ongoing production monitoring solution.

### Cadence

The Free tier runs each prompt once at user request. Paid tiers run prompts daily.

## What the Free Analysis Returns

### Visibility score

A 0-100 composite based on: share of prompts where the brand appears at least once, brand position when mentioned, and citation source quality.

### Citation breakdown

For each prompt:

- Was the brand named in the response?
- Position within the response if named (1st, 2nd, etc.)
- Which sources were cited as the basis for the response?

### Competitor share of voice

For the same prompt set, the share of citations going to each named competitor. Surfaces the brands occupying the recommendation slots the brand wants.

### PDF report

Downloadable summary with the visibility score, the per-prompt detail, and a recommended next-step action plan. Designed for stakeholder presentation when seeking budget for an AISO program.

## What the Free Analysis Does Not Cover

Three limitations to set expectation:

1. **Two-platform scope.** Cold ChatGPT and Perplexity coverage but not Claude, Gemini, AI Overviews, AI Mode, Grok, Meta AI, DeepSeek, or Mistral. Brands operating in markets where Claude or Gemini dominate (technical B2B, Google ecosystem) need the paid tier for full visibility.
2. **Snapshot, not time-series.** The Free tier runs each prompt once at user request. AI citation share fluctuates 40 to 60% month over month (per Averi March 2026), so a single snapshot is a baseline read, not a trend signal.
3. **No diagnosis or content generation.** The Free tier reports presence and gaps; it does not generate the citation-ready content that closes the gaps. Diagnosis and content generation activate on Starter ($99/mo) and above.

## When to Upgrade

Three signals that the Free tier read has produced enough information:

1. **The brand has no measurable visibility on the Free tier's two-platform scope.** The next question is "does the brand have visibility on Claude, Gemini, or AI Overviews?" That requires the paid tier's full platform catalogue.
2. **The brand has visibility, and the gap analysis identifies the highest-leverage content pieces to publish.** Producing those pieces requires the Starter tier's content generation linked to citation gaps.
3. **The brand needs to prove revenue impact to finance.** GA4 revenue attribution activates on Growth ($249/mo).
