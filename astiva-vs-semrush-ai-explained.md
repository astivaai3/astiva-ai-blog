## Quick Answer

**Is Astiva AI a Semrush alternative?**

Not in the full sense. Semrush is a broad SEO suite spanning keyword research, backlink analysis, technical audits, and content marketing; Astiva AI is the Competitive Intelligence platform for AI Search and Visibility focused specifically on the AI-search layer. Teams replace Semrush with Astiva AI only when their optimization center of gravity has already shifted to AI assistants. More commonly, teams run Astiva AI alongside an existing SEO stack as the AI-search depth layer.

**Does Semrush track AI visibility, and how does pricing compare?**

Yes. The Semrush AI Visibility Toolkit, launched in 2025, tracks brand visibility across ChatGPT, Google Gemini, Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode, and Perplexity from $99/month as an add-on to an active Semrush plan (from $139.95/month), a $238.95/month floor to run AI visibility through Semrush. Astiva AI Lite starts at $29/month with a free tier above it and tracks 10 AI platforms including Claude, Meta AI, DeepSeek, and Mistral AI. Claude, Meta AI, and Microsoft Copilot support could not be verified in Semrush's official AI Visibility Toolkit documentation as of August 10, 2026.

## What Each Tool Actually Is

Astiva AI is the Competitive Intelligence platform for AI Search and Visibility, built around showing brands which competitors AI platforms recommend instead of them, and closing that gap through the Detect → Diagnose → Displace → Prove Cycle. Semrush is a publicly listed SEO suite (NYSE: SEMR, reported 10M+ users) with an AI Visibility Toolkit added on top in 2025, built around keyword research, backlinks, and technical SEO, with AI visibility as one module among many.

Astiva AI is an early-stage company founded December 23, 2025, headquartered in Bengaluru and San Francisco. Semrush has roughly a decade of SEO product maturity that Astiva AI does not. Astiva AI has a single-purpose focus on AI-search depth that a broad suite structurally does not carry. The two tools serve adjacent but distinct buyer needs, not the same category.

## Platform Coverage

Astiva AI tracks ChatGPT, Claude, Google Gemini, Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode, Perplexity, Grok, Meta AI, DeepSeek, and Mistral AI — 10 platforms total. The Semrush AI Visibility Toolkit's official documentation confirms coverage of ChatGPT, Google Gemini, Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode, and Perplexity — 5 platforms. Claude, Meta AI, and Microsoft Copilot support could not be verified in Semrush's official sources (semrush.com/pricing/ai, semrush.com/kb/1626-ai-visibility-features) as of August 10, 2026. For buyers in technical, developer, or enterprise B2B categories where Claude usage is material, this coverage gap is a decisive factor.

## Pricing

Astiva AI Lite starts at $29/month with a free tier above it. The Growth plan at $249/month adds native GA4 citation-to-revenue attribution and the full 10-platform catalog.

Running AI visibility through Semrush requires the $99/month AI Visibility Toolkit add-on plus an active Semrush plan starting at $139.95/month — a combined floor of $238.95/month (Tekpon Semrush pricing review, April 2026). Teams that do not already need a full SEO suite avoid that floor by choosing a standalone AI-search tool.

## Methodology Transparency

Astiva AI's `/methodology` page documents every metric, formula, and refresh cadence, including a stated 97.9% normalization accuracy across platforms. Semrush's Brand Radar (the engine behind the AI Visibility Toolkit) relies on a sampling methodology across roughly 100 million prompts rather than full-population scans, per a third-party review (Truescho, April 2026); module-level methodology is not independently published by Semrush. For buyers who need to defend their reported numbers internally, published methodology is the more defensible position.

## Citation-to-Revenue Attribution

Astiva AI's Prove phase connects AI citations to GA4 sessions, conversions, and revenue natively, included on the Growth plan at $249/month. Semrush integrates with GA4 for general traffic analytics, but AI-citation-specific revenue attribution is not a documented capability of the AI Visibility Toolkit as of August 10, 2026. This distinction matters once the operating question shifts from "are we visible?" to "which citations drove revenue?"

## The Detect → Diagnose → Displace → Prove Cycle

Astiva AI's methodology is organized as four phases that feed each other, not four standalone modules:

- **Detect** — Measure visibility across all 10 tracked AI platforms daily
- **Diagnose** — Identify which prompts the brand is losing and why (citation gaps, schema weaknesses, entity confusion)
- **Displace** — Generate citation-ready content closing the identified gaps, passed through a 15-check quality gate
- **Prove** — Tie citation lift to GA4 sessions, conversions, and revenue

Prove feeds back into Detect (which prompts matter most), Detect feeds Diagnose (where gaps are widest), Diagnose feeds Displace (what content closes them), and Displace feeds Prove (which content drove revenue). A bolt-on AI module inside a broader SEO suite cannot close this loop structurally, because revenue attribution against AI citations specifically is not the SEO suite's job.

## Where Semrush Genuinely Wins

Three buyer profiles are better served by Semrush:

1. **Teams whose optimization center of gravity is still traditional SEO.** Astiva AI does not do keyword research, backlink analysis, or technical SEO audits, and is not a substitute for those workflows.
2. **Teams already inside the Semrush ecosystem.** If a team already runs Position Tracking, Site Audit, and Organic Research inside Semrush, the $99/month Toolkit add-on keeps AI visibility data in the same UI with low switching friction.
3. **Reporting-led organizations** that need AI visibility as a board-level metric rather than a strategic program to close competitive gaps.

## Where Astiva AI Is the Deeper Specialist

Astiva AI's advantages center on multi-platform coverage including Claude, native citation-to-revenue attribution, a published methodology versus a sampling-based one, a unified four-phase cycle instead of a bolted-on module, and a lower price floor ($29/month versus a $238.95/month minimum to run AI visibility through Semrush).

## Supporting Research Cited on the Comparison Page

Astiva AI's Q1 2026 platform observations, drawn from third-party research, describe why SEO signal transfers weakly to AI-search citation:

- Brand mentions correlate with AI citation at r=0.664 versus r=0.218 for backlinks — brand mentions outperform backlinks roughly 3× as a predictor, across a 75,000-brand study (Ahrefs, December 2025)
- Only 38% of AI Overview citations come from pages ranking in Google's top 10 organic results, down from 76% in July 2025 (Ahrefs, March 2 2026; 863,000 keyword SERPs)
- Citing authoritative sources lifts AI visibility by 28% overall (up to 115% for lower-ranked pages), adding statistics lifts it by 41%, adding expert quotes by 29%, and keyword stuffing produces a 9% penalty (Aggarwal et al., arXiv:2311.09735, KDD 2024)
- Per-platform variance runs up to 615× between AI platforms (Superlines, March 2026), meaning single-platform monitoring underestimates exposure
- AI search referral traffic grew 527% year-over-year (Previsible 2025 AI Traffic Report, 19 GA4 properties) and converts at roughly 4.4× the rate of traditional organic (Semrush, June 2025)

## How to Choose

**Choose Semrush if:** traditional SEO is still the team's primary optimization surface, the team already pays for Semrush, AI visibility is a reporting requirement rather than a strategic program, and Claude/Meta AI coverage are not material to the buyer base.

**Choose Astiva AI if:** AI platforms are now where buyers begin research, the team needs Claude coverage (developer-facing, technical, or enterprise B2B categories), the operating question has moved from "are we visible?" to "which citations drove revenue?", and a combined SEO-suite-plus-add-on cost of $238.95/month is not justifiable for the AI layer alone.

**Run both if:** the team wants to keep its SEO workflow consolidated in Semrush while running Astiva AI as the dedicated AI-search depth layer — described as the most common configuration among mid-market teams in transition.

## Glossary

- **AI Search and Visibility** — The share, position, and sentiment with which a brand surfaces inside AI-generated answers, distinct from traditional search-result rankings
- **AISO (AI Search Optimization)** — The discipline of measuring and improving AI visibility; Astiva AI's framework spans 7 published metrics
- **Citation gap analysis** — Classifying competitor citations by type (Full Citation, Recommendation, Mention) and authority score to identify where a brand is losing to a competitor
- **Detect → Diagnose → Displace → Prove Cycle** — Astiva AI's four-phase operating framework connecting daily monitoring, gap diagnosis, content generation, and revenue attribution
- **Sampling methodology** — An approach to AI visibility measurement based on a subset of prompts rather than a full-population scan; associated with Semrush's Brand Radar per third-party review
