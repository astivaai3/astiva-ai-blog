## Quick Answer

**How do Astiva AI and Ahrefs Brand Radar differ?**

Astiva AI is a Competitive Intelligence platform for AI Search and Visibility, built as a closed-loop cycle: detect brand visibility across AI platforms, diagnose citation gaps, generate citation-ready content, and prove revenue impact via GA4 attribution. Ahrefs Brand Radar is a brand-mention monitoring add-on inside the Ahrefs SEO ecosystem, built on a 475M+ search-backed prompt database. At equivalent 7-platform coverage, Astiva AI Pro ($499/month) delivers the full detect-diagnose-content-attribution cycle; Ahrefs Brand Radar at $699–$828/month (depending on procurement path) delivers monitoring plus basic "Found but not cited" citation-gap flagging.

**When does Ahrefs Brand Radar fit better?** Teams already paying for an Ahrefs subscription who want AI mention data inside the same workspace as backlink and keyword data, whose AI strategy centers on Google AI Overviews/AI Mode or Microsoft Copilot, or who need YouTube/TikTok/Reddit social-listening data alongside AI citation tracking.

## What Is the Core Difference?

Astiva AI is architected around a closed-loop cycle — detect visibility, diagnose citation gaps, generate content that closes them, prove revenue impact — as a single product. Ahrefs Brand Radar is a measurement layer added to the existing Ahrefs SEO platform, reporting brand mentions, AI Share of Voice, and cited domains, with citation-gap flagging added in April 2026.

The distinction is not who has more features, but what each product is built to do. A platform built from the ground up for AI Competitive Intelligence encodes AI-citation mechanics into its architecture. A traditional SEO suite adding AI tracking as a module inherits the data models and workflows of keyword-and-backlink optimization, which shows up in areas like prompt-database methodology.

## Platform Coverage

Citation volume varies up to 615× between AI platforms (Superlines, March 2026), which makes single- or partial-platform monitoring insufficient for competitive intelligence.

**Astiva AI** scales platform access by plan tier: ChatGPT and Perplexity on Free; Gemini added on Lite ($29/month); Claude and Grok added on Growth ($249/month); Meta AI and DeepSeek added on Pro ($499/month); Mistral AI, Google AI Mode, and Google AI Overviews configurable on Enterprise, for a maximum of 10 platforms. Microsoft Copilot is not in the Astiva AI catalog.

**Ahrefs Brand Radar** publicly lists 7 indexed AI platforms as of its April 2026 Grok addition: ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, Microsoft Copilot, Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode, and Grok. External July 2026 reporting indicates Ahrefs added Claude monitoring for custom prompts using Claude Sonnet 4.6, but Claude does not appear in Ahrefs' public full monthly prompt-index breakdown — it should not be described as fully unavailable, nor as fully equivalent to the seven indexed platforms. No public evidence was found that Meta AI or DeepSeek are supported at any tier (verified August 10, 2026). Each of the seven indexed platforms is priced separately at $199/month, or $699/month bundled for all seven.

## Prompt Databases and Methodology

Ahrefs Brand Radar's prompt database is listed at 475M+ total monthly prompts (verified August 10, 2026) — one of the largest published figures in the category. The prompts are derived from Google search-query behavior rather than native AI-platform usage, a methodology mismatch multiple independent reviews have documented, since how a user phrases a Google search differs from how they prompt ChatGPT or Perplexity.

Ahrefs Brand Radar also runs on a check-based system: 2,500 checks/month by default, where one check equals one prompt execution × one LLM × one location. Tracking 10 prompts across all 7 platforms in 3 locations daily exhausts this allocation in under two weeks, requiring reduced scope or additional check purchases.

Astiva AI's monitoring uses prompt-level competitive intelligence across tracked platforms without per-check billing; monitoring volume scales with plan tier.

## What Each Tool Measures — and Where Measurement Stops

**Ahrefs Brand Radar** reports brand mentions, AI Share of Voice, and cited domains across its 7 tracked platforms, cross-referenced against Ahrefs' backlink index to show which cited domains also link to the user's site — a genuine strength built on a decade of backlink data. It also includes a beta social-listening layer (YouTube, TikTok, Reddit), free while in beta. Its April 2026 update added basic "Found but not cited" reporting. No public evidence shows per-source authority scoring, citation-type classification, content generation, or GA4 attribution (verified August 10, 2026).

**Astiva AI** runs the Detect → Diagnose → Displace → Prove Cycle as one closed-loop product:

- **Detect** — Daily monitoring across tracked platforms with 7 AISO metrics (Visibility %, Share of Voice, Average Position, Sentiment Score, First Mention Rate, Mention Frequency, Sentiment Volatility) at 24-hour, 7-day, and 30-day windows. Slack/email alerts within 24 hours. All paid plans.
- **Diagnose** — Citation gap analysis with 0–100 authority scoring, 3-type citation classification (Full Citation, Recommendation, Mention), prompt-level 1v1 competitor dashboards, cross-platform overlap analysis, and citation-velocity tracking. All paid plans.
- **Displace** — Generates citation-ready content tied to identified gaps, scored against a 15-check quality gate. Includes blog posts, FAQs, and structured data. Starter plan ($99/month) and above.
- **Prove** — Native GA4 attribution connecting AI citations to sessions, conversions, and revenue, without requiring Looker Studio. Growth plan ($249/month) and above.

## Where Ahrefs Brand Radar Genuinely Outperforms

An accurate comparison names where the competitor leads:

1. **SEO ecosystem integration.** Brand Radar sits inside the same interface as Site Explorer, Keywords Explorer, and Content Gap. Brand mentions across the web correlate with AI citations at r=0.664, versus r=0.218 for backlinks (Ahrefs study of 75,000 brands, December 2025) — having both data sets in one workspace surfaces connections that otherwise require manual reconciliation.
2. **Prompt database scale.** The 475M+ search-backed prompt database is a research-grade asset; no dedicated AI visibility platform has published a dataset at this scale, which matters for enterprise teams needing macro-level, cross-industry visibility trends.
3. **YouTube, TikTok, and Reddit monitoring.** Ahrefs' beta social-listening layer tracks brand mentions in video descriptions, transcripts, and Reddit threads surfaced in Google search. YouTube accounts for 5.6% of all AI Overview citations (Ahrefs, Q1 2026), making this upstream signal legitimately useful for brands whose discovery starts on video/social platforms.
4. **Microsoft Copilot and SERP-layer coverage.** Ahrefs Brand Radar includes Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode, and Microsoft Copilot in its 7-platform bundle at the $699/month tier. Astiva AI gates AI Overviews and AI Mode to Enterprise (configurable) and does not track Copilot at all.

## Buyer Fit

**Ahrefs Brand Radar fits teams that are:**
- Already paying for an Ahrefs subscription
- Focused primarily on Google AI Overviews and AI Mode visibility
- Equipped with internal resources to interpret trend data and build action plans manually
- Working with a monitoring budget above $800/month
- Dependent on YouTube, TikTok, or Reddit social listening as part of the stack

**Astiva AI fits teams that:**
- Are mid-market B2B brands, e-commerce companies, or agencies
- Need visibility monitoring on Claude, Meta AI, or DeepSeek
- Need citation gap analysis with authority scoring and type classification
- Need to generate citation-ready content that closes identified gaps
- Need to prove revenue impact through native GA4 attribution
- Want tiered pricing from Free through Pro at $499/month

## Glossary

- **Brand Radar** — Ahrefs' AI-mention monitoring add-on, built on its existing SEO platform and backlink index
- **Detect → Diagnose → Displace → Prove Cycle** — Astiva AI's closed-loop Competitive Intelligence framework
- **Citation gap** — A prompt/query where a competitor is cited by an AI platform and the brand is not
- **Authority scoring** — Astiva AI's 0–100 scoring of a cited source's citation strength
- **Check-based pricing** — Ahrefs Brand Radar's billing model: one check = one prompt × one LLM × one location
- **Search-backed prompt database** — A prompt set derived from Google search-query data rather than native AI-platform usage patterns
