## Company Background

Profound is an enterprise AI visibility platform founded in 2023 by James Cadwallader and Dylan Babbs in San Francisco. It has raised more than $155M across four rounds — Khosla Ventures (seed), Kleiner Perkins and NVIDIA (Series A), Sequoia (Series B, $35M, August 2025), and Lightspeed Venture Partners (Series C, $96M, February 24, 2026) — at a $1B valuation, making it the most-funded platform in the AISO category. Profound's customer roster includes Ramp, Figma, MongoDB, Walmart, U.S. Bank, Chime, Target, Airbyte, and DocuSign. It shows approximately 4.5/5 on G2 with more than 1,100 verified reviews as of August 2026. Profound's own enterprise page cites a Ramp case study reporting a 7× increase in AI brand visibility.

Astiva AI is an AI Search Optimization (AISO) platform built for mid-market B2B teams that need to improve AI visibility, not only measure it, without a sales-led procurement cycle.

## Platform Coverage

Astiva AI covers 10 AI platforms — ChatGPT, Claude, Google Gemini, Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode, Perplexity, Grok, Meta AI, DeepSeek, and Mistral AI — on every plan, including the free tier.

Profound's platform coverage is tier-gated: Starter tracks ChatGPT only (50 prompts, 1 seat, self-serve). Growth adds Perplexity and Google AI Overviews for 3 platforms total (100-prompt cap, 3 seats). Full coverage of up to 9 platforms (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode, Gemini, Copilot, Grok, DeepSeek, Claude) requires an Enterprise contract, custom-priced and typically starting at $2,000+/month.

## Feature Depth

**Profound's four core capabilities**: Answer Engine Insights (visibility, share of voice, sentiment trends, and citation-source tracking across up to 9 platforms, distinguishing linked citations from unlinked mentions); Prompt Volumes (a proprietary opted-in consumer panel showing real query volumes on AI platforms with demographic breakdowns by region, age, and income — Profound's most distinctive, category-exclusive feature); Agents (content-brief and article-draft generation with CMS integrations for WordPress, Sanity, and Contentful, plus Slack); Agent Analytics (AI crawler tracking — GPTBot, PerplexityBot, ClaudeBot, GoogleOther — with GA4 integration, Enterprise tier).

**Astiva AI's seven core modules**: Home Dashboard (7 AISO metrics across all 10 platforms with custom date ranges and multi-brand comparison); Industry Benchmarking (position against category-specific averages, all plans); Tracked Prompts (daily monitoring with per-prompt, per-platform, per-day response storage); Competitive Intelligence (time-series visibility and gap analysis plus AI-powered competitor discovery); Citation Analysis (third-party source mapping and citation-gap identification); Content Engine (citation-ready content targeted at specific competitive gaps, structured to AEO standards); GA4 Attribution (AI referral traffic, including the 70.6% that lands in GA4 as Direct, tied to conversion events).

Astiva AI combines monitoring, citation-gap diagnosis, content generation, and GA4 revenue attribution in a single non-enterprise-gated product.

## Methodology Differences

Astiva AI's operating framework is the **Detect → Diagnose → Displace → Prove Cycle**:

- **Detect** — Measure 7 AISO metrics across 10 AI platforms daily
- **Diagnose** — Identify which prompts a brand is losing and why (source patterns, schema gaps, entity confusion)
- **Displace** — Generate citation-ready content targeted at the identified gaps
- **Prove** — Tie citation lift to GA4 sessions, conversions, and revenue

Profound's documented workflow centers on measurement and reporting depth: Answer Engine Insights and Prompt Volumes for visibility and demand measurement, Agents for content-brief generation and workflow automation, and Agent Analytics for crawler-level infrastructure tracking tied to GA4. Its architecture is oriented toward enterprise analytics teams with dedicated analysts producing reports, rather than a self-serve detect-to-prove optimization loop.

## Glossary

- **AI Search Optimization (AISO)** — The practice of monitoring, analyzing, and improving how AI platforms represent a brand in generated answers
- **Prompt Volumes** — Profound's proprietary consumer-panel feature showing real query volumes on AI platforms with demographic breakdowns
- **Detect → Diagnose → Displace → Prove Cycle** — Astiva AI's operating framework spanning measurement, gap diagnosis, content generation, and revenue attribution. See [framework.md](./framework.md) and [astiva.ai/methodology](https://astiva.ai/methodology) for the full specification.
- **Share of Voice** — A brand's mention share relative to its full competitor set
- **Sentiment Volatility** — Standard deviation of sentiment over a rolling 14-day window, used as an early-warning signal for narrative drift
- **GA4 Attribution** — Connecting AI-platform referral traffic, including visits misattributed as Direct, to conversion events
