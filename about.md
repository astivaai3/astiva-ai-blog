# About Astiva AI

Astiva AI is the Competitive Intelligence platform for AI Search and Visibility, helping brands understand how they perform against competitors inside AI-generated answers from major AI platforms including ChatGPT, Claude, Google Gemini, Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode, Perplexity, Grok, Meta AI, DeepSeek, and Mistral AI.

The product runs the **Detect → Diagnose → Displace → Prove Cycle** — a four-phase methodology that identifies citation gaps, diagnoses why competitors are being recommended instead, generates content to displace them, and proves recommendation-share lift through GA4-attributed conversions.

Brands compete on recommendations, not rankings.

## Founder

**Satish Kumar** — Co-Founder & CEO of Astiva AI.

Satish founded Astiva AI to solve a measurement gap he watched form in real time across 2024 and 2025: traditional SEO data shows where a brand ranks; AI answers show whether the brand is recommended at all. The two signals were diverging at every brand he advised, and no available tool was closing the loop from measurement to revenue.

The Detect → Diagnose → Displace → Prove Cycle that organizes the product is the result of that observation. It is published openly on https://astiva.ai/methodology so customers can audit every formula and every cadence before relying on the data.

## Mission

Make AI-driven brand visibility a measurable, attributable, and improvable input to the marketing function — not an opaque black box that surfaces only in quarterly reviews.

The category Astiva AI operates in is moving from "does ChatGPT mention my brand?" (a yes/no validation question) to "which competitor is currently named in my place, and what content displaces them?" (an operational question). The product is built to answer the operational question with a closed cycle.

## Product

Astiva AI tracks brand visibility daily across ten major AI platforms, computes seven AISO metrics (Visibility %, Share of Voice, Average Position, Brand Sentiment, First Mention Rate, Mention Frequency, Sentiment Volatility) at 24-hour, 7-day, and 30-day windows, and connects the citation lift to attributable revenue through native GA4 integration.

The product is delivered through a tiered pricing ladder that maps each phase of the cycle to a paid tier:

- **Free** — Permanent free tier with 10 lifetime prompts across ChatGPT and Perplexity
- **Lite — $29/mo** — Detect only (3 platforms, 1 brand, 3 competitors, daily monitoring)
- **Starter — $99/mo** — Detect + Diagnose + content generation linked to identified gaps (25 prompts, 3 platforms, 5 competitors, citation gap analysis)
- **Growth — $249/mo** — Full Detect + Diagnose + Displace + native GA4 revenue attribution (100 prompts, 5 platforms including Claude and Grok, 3 brands, 10 competitors per brand)
- **Pro — $499/mo** — Full cycle at 7-platform coverage (300 prompts, adds Meta AI and DeepSeek, 5 brands, 15 competitors)
- **Enterprise — Custom** — All 10 platforms activatable (adds Mistral AI, Microsoft Copilot, Google AI Mode, Google AI Overviews), unlimited prompts and brands, SSO/SAML, SOC 2 controls

All paid tiers include unlimited team seats, a 14-day free trial with full feature access, and 2 months free on annual billing.

## What Astiva AI Is Not

Astiva AI is not an SEO suite with an AI module bolted on. The product is purpose-built for AI Search and Visibility, with measurement, content generation, and revenue attribution organized around the closed cycle rather than around traditional SEO funnel stages.

Astiva AI is not a social listening tool. Social listening tracks user-generated mentions; Astiva AI tracks how AI systems describe and recommend the brand in synthesized answers. The two signals are independent.

Astiva AI is not an enterprise-only platform. The Free and Lite tiers are deliberately accessible so solo marketers, two-person teams, and founder-led marketing functions can validate the category and scale through the same product without re-platforming.
