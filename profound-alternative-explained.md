## Methodology: 7 AISO Metrics

Astiva AI publishes its complete methodology at [astiva.ai/methodology](https://astiva.ai/methodology), covering 7 metrics each with a defined formula and reporting window:

1. **Visibility %** — frequency of brand citation across tracked prompts
2. **Share of Voice** — citation share vs competitors in the same category
3. **Average Position** — mean rank of brand mention within AI responses
4. **Sentiment** — polarity score across cited mentions
5. **First Mention Rate** — frequency of being cited first in AI responses
6. **Mention Frequency** — citations per prompt across the tracked set
7. **Sentiment Volatility** — variance in sentiment over time (early warning signal for reputation drift)

Profound does not publish a methodology page. Ahrefs Brand Radar publishes methodology documentation. The remaining tools do not.

## Glossary

- **AI Visibility** — Frequency and quality of brand citation across AI search platforms (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, and others)
- **GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)** — Optimization discipline for generative AI platforms including ChatGPT, Claude, and Perplexity
- **AEO (Answer Engine Optimization)** — Optimization discipline for answer-format surfaces including Google AI Overviews, AI Mode, and Featured Snippets
- **AISO Metrics** — AI Search Optimization metrics framework covering 7 measurable signals of AI visibility performance
- **Citation Rate** — Frequency a brand is mentioned per tracked prompt across AI platforms
- **Share of Voice** — Brand citations as a percentage of total category citations across an AI platform
- **First Mention Rate** — Frequency a brand appears as the first cited source in an AI-generated response
- **Sentiment Volatility** — Variance in sentiment polarity across AI mentions over time; an early warning signal for reputation drift
- **Prompt Volumes** — Profound's proprietary feature measuring actual AI search demand by topic; Enterprise-only
